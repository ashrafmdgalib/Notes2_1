    \chapter*{Syllabus}
    \thispagestyle{empty}
    \vspace*{-2.7cm}
    \textbf{PN Junction Diode and Diode Circuits:} Semiconductor Materials, Construction
of PN Junction, Formation of Depletion Layer and Barrier Voltage in PN Junction,
Current-Voltage Characteristics of a PN Junction Diode, Equivalent Circuit of
Diode, Resistance and Capacitance of PN Junction, Diode Circuit and Load Line,
Zener Diode Operation and Application, Half-Wave and Full-Wave Rectifier, Voltage Multiplier, Clipper and Clamper. \\ [5pt]
\textbf{Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT):} Construction and Operation of BJT,
Amplifying Action, Characteristics of BJT in Common Base (CB), Common
Emitter (CE) and Common Collector (CC) Configurations, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ , Q-Point and Load Line, Different Biasing Circuits, Stability Factor, BJT as a Switch, re and
Hybrid Equivalent Circuit of BJT. \\ [5pt]
\textbf{Single Stage BJT Amplifier Circuits: }Operation of Single-Stage Amplifier,
Voltage and Current Gain, Input and Output Impedance of CB, CE, CC
Configurations using h-Parameter, Effect of Unbypassed $R_E$, $R_S$. \\ [5pt]
\textbf{Field Effect Transistor (FET): }JFET Structure, Operation and Characteristics, hParameters for JFET, MOSFET Construction, Operation and Characteristics,
Biasing Circuits of JFET and MOSFET, Single-stage JFET Amplifier, MOSFET as
a Switch, CMOS Inverter. \\ [5pt]
\textbf{Multistage and Differential Amplifiers: } RC Coupled Two Stage Amplifiers,
Direct-coupled Amplifier, Darlington Pair, Multistage Frequency Effects. \\ [5pt]
\textbf{Feedback Amplifiers: } Principle of Feedback Amplifier, Positive and Negative
Feedback, Advantages of Negative Feedback, Gain Stability, Decreased Distortion,
Increased Bandwidth, Forms of Negative Feedback, Practical Feedback Circuits.
